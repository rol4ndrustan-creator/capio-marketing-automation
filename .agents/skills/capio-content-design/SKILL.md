# CAPIO Content Design

**Read the repository's `content-design.md` in full before any CAPIO artwork work.**

## Trigger
Use this skill for requests to create, edit, review, render, compare or upload CAPIO carousel and visual marketing content. It also applies when maintaining the CAPIO Content OS renderer.

## Procedure
1. Inspect the live CAPIO website, original logo, prior founder-approved reference PNGs and post-specific source documents. Never claim to have seen files you cannot retrieve.
2. Read `content-design.md`; use its website-grounded design tokens, thematic guidance and prohibitions.
3. Create a short creative brief and TWO distinct cover concepts for each topic. Seek founder visual approval before producing the remaining slides.
4. Follow the approved art direction in rendering editable individual 1080x1350 PNGs, coherent captions and accurate claims.
5. Perform visual and factual QA; do not equate image rendering with content approval.
6. Preserve old Drive assets and use versioned filenames when new drafts are uploaded.
7. Never deploy, schedule or publish solely because an image exists.

## Required reporting
Identify the selected theme, original references, exact created assets, dimension tests, editorial/factual review status, and verified Drive destination. If unavailable, declare the blocker rather than invent completion.
