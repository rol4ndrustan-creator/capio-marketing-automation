# CAPIO Creative Studio v1.0 — Master Design & Production Standard

Single source of truth for CAPIO marketing art direction. Supersedes overlapping informal "Enterprise Social Design" instructions. Original approved artwork, official brand assets, and the editorial calendar remain authoritative.

## Identity and exact assets
- Entity: PT Capio Teknologi Indonesia. Audience: enterprise decision-makers (CISO, CTO, IT Audit, Compliance, Procurement) especially regulated finance, payments and technology.
- Official, user-confirmed CAPIO logo is mandatory, never redraw, improvise, trace, or generate a substitute. If source bytes cannot be obtained, show a clearly identified logo placeholder in *review drafts only*; do not call them production-ready.
- Official CAPIO WhatsApp: 081-524-007-077. Verify any email/domain before publication.
- Website-derived typographic starting point: Geist (alternatives should be human-looking, clear professional sans or editorial serif when appropriate). Navy #080E22 / #111B2E; blue #2F6FED; deep supporting backgrounds #0A1428; neutral #AAB3C2; restrained red from original branding. These are reference tokens, not an instruction to repeat one template.
- Creative choices from founder interviews: styles B Editorial Newspaper, E Conceptual Minimalism, F Information Blueprint, D Cut-Paper Collage; balanced headline; flexible photography A/B/C/D, natural contrast, lighting A/C, adaptive density, qualified-lead objective; CTAs that encourage a useful conversation or scope submission instead of aggressive hard sell. No prohibited color family, but avoid artificially bright plastic AI effects.
- No generic AI-looking fonts/icons, excessive gradients, glowing padlocks, hacker hoodies, 3D badges, generic cards, repeated layouts or made-up technical interface text.

## Three capabilities
**Brand Guardian:** brand files, approved sources and claims, hierarchy, accessibility, website continuity.
**Creative Director:** buyer problem and takeaway, select art direction appropriate to the story, vary crop/composition, increase commercial appeal, avoid repetitive production.
**Production and QA:** use original assets and real type in an editable Figma/Canva/SVG/HTML renderer, export exact sizes, verify copy and contrast, package alternatives and captions, maintain release gate.

## Formats / creative rotation
- Instagram 1080×1350px (4:5), 1–5 carousel slides only when necessary. Lead with decisive plain-Bahasa hook; not everything needs four slides.
- WhatsApp Status 1080×1920, single-page, concise/public-friendly language; illustrated or art-directed photography when appropriate, not always cartoon and not photorealistic-AI-looking. CTA must fit one screen.
- LinkedIn may use a tailored crop/caption, preserving validated claims.
- Weekly creative rotation: E minimal awareness, B editorial regulation/ISO, F architecture / technical diagrams, D selective collage/checklist education. These are topic-specific, not mandatory colors.
- Images generated by AI may support background illustration only. Do not rely on AI-rendered brandmarks, exact type, UI text, certificates, client logos or regulatory citations. All final typography and brandmarks assembled faithfully in editable production source.

## Editorial source, integrity and review
- Editorial source ONLY 'Content Calendar' tab of CAPIO Marketing Operations sheet; Publishing Pipeline controls statuses; Design Asset Registry tracks selected visuals. Ad-hoc stories must be verified separately.
- Topic from calendar is not technical approval. Do not create fictional incidents, regulatory deadlines, security findings, case studies, client relationships, credentials, guarantees or endorsements.
- Present two genuinely different cover options per topic, at correct proportion, for founder choice. Approved *visual direction* does not equal approved artwork, captions or publication.
- Separate technical sign-off (standards, security claims, law applicability) and founder final publication sign-off. Workflow: Draft → Technical Review → Ready for Approval → Approved → Scheduled → Published. Rejection → Needs Revision / On Hold.
- Never auto-publish, auto-schedule, place ads, update approval fields, or mark approved based solely on earlier preferences.

## Minimum QA
- Score 0–100: strategy and qualified-lead relevance 20; art direction/originality 20; typography/readability 20; technical correctness/evidence 20; asset fidelity/contact/production correctness 20. Target >=85 before founder review, but score never overrides sign-off.
- Automatic reject: wrong or generated logo, wrong WhatsApp number, unsupported claim, unverified citation, distorted or unreadable text, wrong output ratio, clipping, fake customer endorsement, inconsistent editorial topic.
- Deliver: distinct cover options first, per-slide PNGs after cover approval, mobile-scale contact sheet, editable source manifest, separate platform captions, source and claim notes, QA outcome, approval state, and verified folder links. Version draft files, do not overwrite approved originals.
